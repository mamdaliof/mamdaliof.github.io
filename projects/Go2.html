Unitree Go2 Quadruped LiDAR & Visual SLAM

Fast-LIO2 and Point-LIO LiDAR Odometry, Nav2 3D Elevation Costmaps, and Multi-Sensor Fusion

Project Overview

The Unitree Go2 is an agile quadruped robot platform. In this project, I implemented low-drift Simultaneous Localization and Mapping (SLAM) and autonomous path planning across challenging indoor and outdoor 3D environments.

Unitree Go2 Quadruped Fast-LIO2 & Point-LIO ROS2 Nav2 Costmaps Intel RealSense Depth + IMU

The Challenge

Legged robots experience sharp body oscillations during trotting and stair climbing. Standard 2D SLAM and simple visual odometry quickly drift or fail completely when traversing uneven, unpaved, or low-texture terrain.

The Engineering Solution

I deployed a tightly-coupled direct LiDAR-inertial SLAM pipeline:

  • Fast-LIO2 and Point-LIO: Directly registered raw 3D LiDAR points with high-rate IMU measurements via an incremental k-d tree (ikd-Tree), eliminating feature extraction bottlenecks and keeping drift under 0.15 meters over 100 meters of travel.
  • Nav2 3D Elevation Mapping: Converted dense point clouds into 2.5D elevation grid maps to calculate traversability gradients, letting the quadruped navigate steps and slopes safely.
  • Visual Geometry Grounding: Integrated front-facing depth cameras to refine obstacle avoidance in close-proximity blind spots.
