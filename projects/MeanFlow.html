MeanFlow 1-Step Generative Trajectory Policy

Continuous Normalizing Flows and 1-Step Flow Matching via JVP for 10x Faster Robot Action Generation

Project Overview

Generative diffusion models excel at learning multi-modal robot behaviors, but their step-by-step sampling creates latency bottlenecks. MeanFlow is a flow matching policy architecture that generates full robotic action trajectories in a single forward pass, achieving sub-4ms decision cycles on challenging manipulation benchmarks like Push-T.

1-Step Flow Matching 3.6 ms Inference Latency Jacobian-Vector Products (JVP) Push-T Benchmark

The Challenge

Standard diffusion policies require 20 to 100 sequential denoising steps per action chunk. In closed-loop robotic control, this introduces 50 to 200 ms of latency per step, which is too slow for dynamic obstacle avoidance or fast contact tasks.

The Engineering Solution

I formulated and implemented a 1-step trajectory generation framework:

  • Continuous Normalizing Flows (CNF): Modeled action distributions as straight probability paths between random Gaussian noise and expert demonstration chunks.
  • Jacobian-Vector Product (JVP) Optimization: Enforced straight-flow velocity vector fields during training using forward-mode automatic differentiation, allowing single-step Euler integration at inference.
  • Multimodal Retention: Preserved the ability to capture distinct multimodal strategies (such as pushing from the left versus the right) while eliminating sampling loops.
