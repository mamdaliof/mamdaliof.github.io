YoDINO Zero-Annotation Battery Anomaly Detection

DINOv3 Vision Foundation Models, Parameter-Efficient Bottleneck Adapters and Unsupervised Isolation Forest

Project Overview

In battery manufacturing, rare defects cannot be anticipated with large supervised training sets. YoDINO is a computer vision research pipeline that combines self-supervised DINOv3 vision foundation models with parameter-efficient fine-tuning (PEFT) and unsupervised anomaly scoring to detect novel battery defects with zero prior defect annotations.

DINOv3 Foundation Model Bottleneck PEFT Adapters Isolation Forest Anomaly 0.849 Unsupervised AUROC

The Challenge

Production lines produce millions of normal parts but few defective examples. Supervised classification fails when new, unseen defect types emerge, and fine-tuning billions of parameters in foundation models is computationally impractical on manufacturing edge devices.

The Engineering Solution

I formulated a two-tier foundation vision architecture:

  • Parameter-Efficient Bottleneck Adapters: Inserted linear bottleneck adapters into intermediate Vision Transformer layers, freezing 99.1% of backbone weights while achieving 91.13% classification accuracy.
  • Zero-Annotation Isolation Forest: Extracted patch-level self-supervised embeddings from normal battery surfaces and fitted an unsupervised Isolation Forest, scoring anomalies without requiring defect labels.
  • Edge Inference: Pre-computed feature dictionaries for millisecond-level anomaly heatmaps on the line.
