Automated AI Hemorrhage Triage & CT-as-Video

The Clinical Challenge

Emergency physicians and on-call radiologists evaluate non-contrast head CT series under extreme time pressure. Class imbalance is severe (bleeding lesions occupy less than 2% of total scan volumes), and isolated 2D slice analysis often misses subtle hematoma margins and ventricle tracking.

Spatio-Temporal Sequence Modeling (CT-as-Video)

To replicate how clinicians scroll through sequential slices to recognize anatomical flow, we modeled the 3D CT axial stack as continuous video. Using a hybrid CNN-Transformer pipeline, the model aggregates slice-level features with temporal self-attention across the whole scan volume.

Explainable AI, Weakly-Supervised & Representation Learning

The Annotation Bottleneck

Pixel-level segmentation of 3D CT scans requires hundreds of hours of manual annotation by senior neuroradiologists. To break this dependency, we engineered self-supervised representation learning and weakly-supervised localization architectures.

Self-Supervised & Generative AI Priors

By pre-training foundation visual encoders on unlabeled CT scan volumes, the network learns rich representations of healthy brain anatomy. Generative priors capture anatomical symmetry and tissue density distributions, allowing downstream classifiers to detect hemorrhage anomalies with zero or minimal fine-grained segmentation masks.

Representation Learning Pipeline
  • Self-Supervised Pre-training: Contrastive feature representations trained across 3D volumes without manual slice labels.
  • Weakly-Supervised Localization: Extracts lesion boundaries directly from scan-level binary labels using Class Activation Maps.
  • Fuzzy Aggregation Rules: Integrates multi-slice attention scores into patient-level risk predictions, ensuring near-zero false-negative safety.

Hemorrica 300+ Dataset & 3D Morphometry

Hemorrica Dataset Overview

Curated and standardized the Hemorrica dataset, encompassing over 300 multi-slice non-contrast head CT scan volumes. Each volume was labeled and multi-verified across all 5 clinical hemorrhage subtypes: Intraparenchymal (IPH), Intraventricular (IVH), Subarachnoid (SAH), Subdural (SDH), and Epidural (EDH).
